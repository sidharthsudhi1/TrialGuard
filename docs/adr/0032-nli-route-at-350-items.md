# AD-32 — The NLI route re-tested at 350 items

> Part of the TrialGuard architectural decision log ([index](../../README.md#architectural-decision-log)). Amendments are appended here rather than replacing what they correct.

## Decision

At 350 items the NLI route does not rank respectably, let alone threshold, and the prerequisite for the human labelling pass inverts. AD-23 rejected `deberta-large-mnli` on 50 items as ranking well (AUC 0.79) but uncalibratable, and AD-29 closed its named biomedical variable the same way. Re-run against **350 items** (50 human, 300 model-adjudicated, disjoint and asserted so), PubMedBERT-MedNLI falls from **AUC 0.7648 to 0.5758** (bootstrap CI [0.512, 0.642], barely off chance) and DeBERTa-v3-large-fever-anli sits at 0.6071 [0.547, 0.664]. Best precision across every threshold is 0.369 and 0.435 against a base rate of 0.297, each bought by destroying sound citations in bulk -- MedNLI's best point rejects 130 items and destroys 82 sound ones. **The confound leads rather than trails**: 86% of the labels are model-generated and measured lenient (AD-29), so this is not a human-validated result. Splitting by label source is deliberately inconclusive -- MedNLI's human and model intervals barely fail to overlap while DeBERTa-v3's overlap substantially, which is weak evidence for a label-source effect and equally consistent with the n=50 figures having been small-sample overestimates, since both human CIs are wide enough to contain the model point estimates. The parsimonious reading is regression toward chance. What changes is the order of operations: a human pass was the prerequisite for **finding** a threshold and is now the test of whether one **could exist**, so the recommendation is a targeted 100-item re-label rather than several hundred more, answering sample-size-versus-label-source and threshold-existence together at a third of the effort. `deberta-large-mnli` itself was not re-run -- its repository publishes no safetensors and the .bin needs ~1.6 GB against 1.2 GB free -- so its 0.7899 stands as prior and no n=350 figure is claimed for it. [`e7_nli350_findings.md`](../../data/reports/e7_nli350_findings.md)

## Alternatives considered

Quoting the 350-item AUC as the answer (86% of the labels are the model's own and lenient; AD-15 rejected exactly this substitution); reading the MedNLI human-vs-model gap as proof that model labels destroy the signal, when one comparison is marginal and the other overlaps; funding several hundred more adjudications to find a threshold, when the evidence now argues for testing whether one exists at all
