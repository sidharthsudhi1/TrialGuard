# AD-29 — A biomedical NLI checkpoint is still not shippable

> Part of the TrialGuard architectural decision log ([index](../../README.md#architectural-decision-log)). Amendments are appended here rather than replacing what they correct.

## Decision

A biomedical NLI checkpoint separates the entailment classes far better and is still not shippable, so AD-23's loose end is closed rather than open. AD-23 rejected `deberta-large-mnli` as a second verifier and named one untried variable: a biomedical checkpoint, since MNLI premises are sentences while these are clinical fragments. Tested on the same 50 adjudicated items, `PubMedBERT-MNLI-MedNLI` **supports that diagnosis** -- median sound/unsound separation rises from 4.1x to 18.2x -- and **refutes the hope built on it**: its best point is precision 0.60 at p<0.002, catching 12 of 18 unsound citations while destroying 8 sound ones, and **no checkpoint has any operating point that catches anything without destroying sound citations**, which is the bar because the product claim is that a GROUNDED badge means something. A larger modern general NLI model (`DeBERTa-v3-large-mnli-fever-anli-ling-wanli`) is the worst of the three at AUC 0.6901, which rules out "use a better NLI model" as cleanly as the biomedical result rules out "use an in-domain one". Ranking improved, usability did not follow. The second half of E7 is the blocker AD-23 actually named: WS-5b's 50 items were drawn ad hoc and only the output survived, so there was no sampler to extend and no merge step for a second rater. Both now exist (`scripts/e7_entailment_sample.py`), **300 items are drawn** stratified by cohort and kind -- deliberately not proportional, since a proportional draw swamps exclusion criteria and AD-19 put the sharpest slice of this gap on exclusion `not_met` -- with the population held comparable to WS-5b at 6.7% absence-grounded against 6.0%. The draw runs cached-only and costs nothing. **Nothing was auto-labelled**: AD-3 rules out an LLM judge for enforcement and AD-15 already rejected using one offline to size the problem, so the worksheet ships with 300 nulls and the honest state is that the engineering is done and the judgment is not. [`e7_findings.md`](../../data/reports/e7_findings.md)

## Alternatives considered

Auto-filling the 300 `entails` fields with an LLM (produces a large gold set carrying exactly the property that makes the existing 50 worth more than it); shipping MedNLI at p<0.002 for its 12-of-18 catch rate, which destroys 8 sound citations and weakens the badge in the way L4's abstention laundering weakened the faithfulness proxy; declaring the NLI route dead on one checkpoint, which is what AD-23 explicitly declined to do and why the biomedical variable was named rather than dismissed
